part of that licence.

You may:
- use the names to refer to this software, truthfully ("built on Tin Hat", "a Tin Hat fork");
- build and run the unmodified source, including under the Tin Hat name;
- ship the Flatpak, package or installer we publish, unchanged.

You may not, without written permission:
- distribute a modified build under the Tin Hat name or with the hat mark;
- use the names or the mark for a service, product or organisation in a way that suggests we made,

- register domains, accounts or trademarks containing "Tin Hat" in relation to chat or calling
  software.

Forks: pick your own name and mark; the notices the AGPL requires already preserve credit.
Questions and permissions: open an issue in this repository (TinHatLabs/TinHat).
